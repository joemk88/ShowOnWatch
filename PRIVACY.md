# Privacy policy for Show on Watch

_Last updated: 17 November 2026_

Show on Watch is a free app made by an independent developer (joemk88). It lets you send a
photo or video from your Android phone to your own Wear OS watch.

## The short version

Show on Watch does not collect, store, share or sell any personal data. It has no accounts,
no ads, no analytics and no tracking. Nothing you send is ever uploaded to the developer or to
any server.

## What the app does with your photos and videos

- **Only what you choose.** The app only reads a photo or video when you share it to
  Show on Watch or pick it in the app. It does not browse your gallery or files.
- **Prepared on your phone.** The phone app makes a smaller copy suitable for a watch
  screen. Any temporary copy is deleted as soon as sending finishes.
- **Sent directly to your watch.** The copy goes straight to your paired watch using the
  Wear OS connection built into Google Play services (Bluetooth or Wi-Fi between your own
  devices).
- **Kept only on your watch.** The watch app keeps just the most recent item in its private
  storage so it can show it again when you reopen the app. Each new item replaces the
  previous one, and everything is removed if you uninstall the app.

## Permissions

- **Notifications:** to show a progress notification while a video is being prepared and sent.
- **Foreground service:** so sending can finish if you switch to another app.

## Third-party services

The app uses Google Play services on your phone and watch to deliver items between your own
devices. That connection is part of Wear OS and is covered by
[Google's privacy policy](https://policies.google.com/privacy). Show on Watch itself sends no
data to Google or anyone else.

## Children

The app is not directed at children and does not knowingly collect information from anyone.

## Changes

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted here with a new date.
